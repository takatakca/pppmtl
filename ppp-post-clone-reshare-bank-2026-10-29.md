## 4. PACING PLAN — professional, no spam dump, no account flags

### 4A. Why a bulk dump is not professional (and why Pierre's "thousand" is a library goal, not a one-night target)

- Meta, Instagram, TikTok, X, and LinkedIn all flag rapid, repetitive, identical-text posts with links — especially from an account with 0–529 followers, 0–1 posts on several platforms, and a recent rate-limit (X) and bot-mitigation history (TikTok). A 100-post night on one page, or the same text on 10 platforms in the same hour, risks restriction, reduced reach, or a new rate-limit — the opposite of visibility.
- The filed notes already show the cost of rushing: X rate-limited on 2026-10-29, TikTok write API rejecting edits, Yelp silently dropping a toggle, Foursquare reCAPTCHA quota. The plan below is built to avoid adding a fifth platform block.
- **Pierre's "at least a thousand" is therefore treated as a CUMULATIVE LIBRARY goal:** total live items across all PPP pages/platforms (Facebook posts + Instagram posts + Threads posts + Pinterest pins + Google Posts + X posts + LinkedIn posts + TikTok videos + Snapchat stories + YouTube videos), counted once per live item, over months — NOT 1,000 posts on one page in one night, and NOT 1,000 clones of the same 100 Facebook posts posted identically everywhere on the same day.
